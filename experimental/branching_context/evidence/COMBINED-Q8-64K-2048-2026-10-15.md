[suffix-frontier audit](Q8-SUFFIX-FRONTIER-2026-10-15.md), which passed both

2,044 tokens and matched A's uninterrupted final state. Saved parent bytes
remained unchanged. Both branches reached the output bound; neither hit EOS.

across restart. Tested controllers are greedy: accepted count 2,048, next RNG
